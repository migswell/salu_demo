How Salu works, and how we handle your information.

How Salu works

  1. Create or join a fund. Set your savings goal, the number of members and the schedule. The goal is split evenly into each member’s contribution.
  2. The order is set once. Fair Draw picks the payout order at random, or the group agrees on it. Then it locks.
  3. Everyone pays in. Auto-Hulog collects each contribution on the due date.
  4. The money stays locked. Contributions go into the Salu Pot, held by a partner bank. No member can withdraw it.
  5. The pot is paid out. The member whose turn it is receives the full pot. This repeats until everyone has had a turn.

Common questions

What if someone misses a payment?

They get reminders and a few days' grace. If they still don't pay, the Salo Fund tops up the pot so that turn's member is paid in full and on time. The member who missed owes Salu, not the group, and their Salu Score drops.

Can I edit or delete a fund?

Yes. Open the fund and choose Edit fund or Delete fund. Once a contribution has been paid, the goal, members and schedule are locked so the rules stay fair for everyone.

What is the service fee for?

Salu adds 1.5% to each contribution. Part of it builds the Salo Fund. There is no interest and no other charge.

What is the Salu Score?

A record of your on-time payments across groups. A good score lets you take earlier turns in new groups.

Can I change my turn?

Only if every member of the fund approves the swap.

Who can join my fund?

Only people you invite with your fund's link. Salu has no public funds.

Privacy notice

We only ask for what a group needs to run:

  • your full name, to verify your account (never shown to your groups)
  • your nickname, which is how your groups see you; you can change it in your profile
  • your mobile number, for sign-in and payment reminders
  • which wallet Auto-Hulog uses (we show only the last 4 digits)
  • your funds’ goals, schedules and payment history

Other members see your nickname, payments and turn. They never see your full name, mobile number or wallet details.

In this demo: accounts are simulated. Nothing is sent anywhere, everything is saved only in this browser, and no real money moves.
